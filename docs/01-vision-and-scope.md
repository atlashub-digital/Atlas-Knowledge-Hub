# AKH-001 · Purpose, taxonomy and opportunity

**Status:** PROPOSED · **Revision:** 2026-10-10

## Strategic problem
AtlasHub operates corporate decisions, multiple projects, licensed/approved media assets, reusable workforce skills, country-specific domain knowledge and private customer sources. A single folder tree, shared LLM memory or universal Notion database cannot enforce the required ownership and retrieval boundaries.

## Mission
A shared **knowledge service** that catalogs external canonical sources, tracks provenance and access, produces searchable derived representations, and answers with traceable excerpts. It must be portable to managed AI Workforce or client Build/Transfer deployments.

## Explicit non-goals
- Replacing Notion as editorial decision hub, GitHub as code truth, Drive as initial DAM, or AMF Factory's operational database.
- Training/fine-tuning foundation models by default. RAG plus tools/skills is the baseline; model fine-tuning is a separate evaluated initiative.
- One shared raw knowledge repository for all tenants.
- A new commercial business line, a new agent harness, a second workflow engine, or a dependency on PaperClip.

## Primary users
Engineering/Security; corporate leadership; AMF Duarte/Laura/Rui under AMF's permissions; AI Workforce agents; venture-specific staff; external clients with dedicated tenants; document owners and reviewers.

## Use cases
AKH-UC01 approved AtlasHub policies → team/agent grounded answers.
AKH-UC02 index documents from opted-in Notion/Drive/GitHub sources.
AKH-UC03 find AMF creative by campaign, rights, version, status without copying its master.
AKH-UC04 assemble real-estate pack + PT/BR jurisdiction + client listings (live via CRM).
AKH-UC05 assemble medical receptionist + administrative knowledge + clinic scope, with clinical advice prohibition.
AKH-UC06 optional Device Bridge capture of **selected** folders/files after on-device user consent.
AKH-UC07 delete/revoke a source and propagate to search cache, index and agent access.
AKH-UC08 export client's permitted knowledge and deployment bindings for Build & Transfer.

## Business case (assumptions, not claims)
Reduce repeated knowledge preparation, prevent retrieval leakage, accelerate onboarding and reuse curated domain libraries. Test against setup hours, freshness, citation precision, rights violations and cost/query. See [evaluation](12-evaluation.md).
